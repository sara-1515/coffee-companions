Our Story

Born out of my deep love for coffee, this website is my very first creation as a web developer—a space designed for fellow coffee lovers to explore and celebrate the world of specialty coffee. Inspired by the journey of Coffee Companions, which was founded in 2025 by passionate enthusiasts in Portland, I wanted to build something that not only honors their mission of ethical sourcing and rich storytelling, but also reflects my own passion for design, development, and great brews. From bean to cup, every page here is a blend of tradition, craftsmanship, and the joy of learning something new—both in coffee and in code.

Ethiopian Yirgacheffe

This light-roasted coffee offers a delightful complexity with bright citrus notes and floral undertones. The distinctive blueberry aroma is unmistakable, and it finishes with a pleasant tea-like quality. Perfect for pour-over brewing methods that highlight its delicate flavors.

Colombian Supremo

A well-balanced medium roast with nutty undertones and caramel sweetness. The chocolate notes come through nicely with minimal acidity, making it approachable for daily drinking. Works beautifully in both drip machines and French press.

Colombian Supremo

This dark roast delivers a full-bodied experience with earthy, woody characteristics. The low acidity and hints of spice make it distinctive, while its rich, syrupy body stands up well to milk. Best for espresso or French press brewing.

Jamaican Blue Mountain

A premium coffee known for its exceptional smoothness and clean taste profile. Offers subtle notes of chocolate and a mild, sweet flavor with virtually no bitterness. The balanced acidity and nutty undertones make every sip a luxurious experience.
